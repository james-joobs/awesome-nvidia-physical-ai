## Example data and vehicle

PandaSet is by **Scale AI and Hesai**: [author paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.12610), [official devkit](https://github.com/scaleapi/pandaset-devkit). The JPEG-only downloader uses the same pinned **unofficial community mirror** as the [RGB comparison lab](../rgb-reality-view/SOURCES.md). Only camera pixels and the original license are fetched. Matching frame numbers define nominal times; no sensor timestamp, pose or calibration files are read.

The original payload says **CC BY 4.0 + PandaSet Dataset Terms**; [Scale's Nucleus notice](https://scale.com/legal/nucleus-os) has also identified PandaSet as CC BY-SA 4.0. We retain both notices and apply **CC BY-SA 4.0 + original Dataset Terms** to the new scene, resized RGB previews and annotated recordings. Do not imply endorsement or identify individuals. The devkit's Apache license is not the data license. [Original data terms](evidence/PANDASET-DATA-LICENSE.txt).

## Presentation and measured scope

The ego-centered road-user presentation refers to [Tesla's official visualization documentation](https://www.tesla.com/ownersmanual/modely/en_us/GUID-2CB60804-9CEA-4F4B-8B04-09B991368DC5.html). That source does not establish the internal architecture of the newly discussed Reality View. No Tesla source, proprietary model or driving system is reproduced or endorsed here.

The scene is static, and moving traffic can ghost. Novel views can expose gaps; Harmonizer may fill or alter details. Temporal context reduces a stability proxy but can smooth details or retain old appearance; discontinuous viewpoints reset history. Perception uses original RGB, not generated enhancements. Its scores are model predictions, not verified accuracy. Ground contacts assume one RGB-derived road plane.

Alpamayo uses a disclosed virtual-camera adapter, nominal source timing, planar/smoothed RGB-derived ego history and an assumed metric scale. It runs open-loop on four keyframes and does not actuate or evaluate a vehicle. Generated explanations may be incorrect. Separate stage receipts, frame-pair hashes, output shapes, artifact hashes and browser interactions support execution/integrity, not driving safety or hidden-surface truth.

The user-provided Tesla commentary is an architectural hypothesis, not a primary technical specification. [The linked X post](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2124458557525397780) returned 403 during verification. Shared-UVE, mean-flow, model-size, eight-frame and 36fps claims are not asserted for our implementation. [Actual temporal receipts](evidence/temporal/temporal.json) report 1024×576 model-forward timings separately from browser and transport. All new scene derivatives and recordings retain the PandaSet CC BY-SA 4.0 + original Dataset Terms treatment above.

## Execution optimization (2026-11-25)

The current-frame adapter preserves the complete five-frame encoder and DiT input and applies the full latent normalization before decoding the causal first output. It does not change learned weights, resolution, BF16 precision or diffusion steps. [Matched 160-frame receipts](evidence/speed/benchmark.json) and [actual sequential browser traces](evidence/speed/interaction.json) report model-only and capture-to-paint timings separately. Optional channels-last convolution/kernel tuning has small numerical differences and is not the exact-pixel default.

Response encoding uses Pillow lossless WebP (`lossless=True, method=0, quality=0`) when supported, with PNG fallback. The quality argument controls effort in this lossless mode; [Pillow reference](https://pillow.readthedocs.io/en/stable/handbook/image-file-formats.html#webp). Pixel equality is validated on raw outputs, including an actual HTTP/GPU-history check. MP4s are separate presentation encodings. No additional model weights or gated downloads are required. Earlier assets, recordings and experiments remain available.
