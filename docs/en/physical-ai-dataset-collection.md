# NVIDIA Physical AI dataset collection

[Catalog](../../README.md) · [한국어](../ko/physical-ai-dataset-collection.md)

A discovery entry point for NVIDIA physical AI datasets across robotics, driving and other tasks.

**Official sources:** [Collection](https://huggingface.co/collections/nvidia/physical-ai) · [All NVIDIA datasets](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/datasets)

**License and access:** Dataset terms and access vary by entry. Models, data, dependencies and hosted services may have separate terms; check each source before use.
