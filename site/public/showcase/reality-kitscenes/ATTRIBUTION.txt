Frankfurt RGB novel-view reconstruction, for non-commercial educational use.

Original camera imagery: KITScenes Multimodal, Institute of Measurement and
Control Systems (MRT), Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT), and FZI Research
Center for Information Technology. https://kitscenes.org/multimodal/
Schwarzkopf, Immel et al., The Road Ahead in Autonomous Driving:
The KITScenes Multimodal Dataset. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02956

Source of the camera files: the public FiftyOne release prepared by Harpreet
Sahota and Voxel51, revision e6cdac8eb56b2a8ee329cd31b16800cda53ef3e0.
Downtown bridge: 00fc1b9b-005e-554c-7733-c4c0adeca015.
Tram and intersections: 03ed2585-0729-92ad-98af-fdd9ac1c3a80.
Tree-lined avenue: 04a39028-c434-6cff-b3f4-9f8dbb9aa078.
https://huggingface.co/datasets/Voxel51/kitscenes-multimodal

The reconstructed scenes, road and sky textures, downscaled RGB frames,
videos and posters use the
source data's CC BY-NC 4.0 license and KITScenes dataset terms.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/
https://huggingface.co/datasets/KIT-MRT/KITScenes-Multimodal

Changes: sampled six anonymized RGB camera streams, resampled images,
applied one tone curve, estimated poses with LoMa-B and COLMAP, excluded
dynamic/sky regions from static appearance fitting, fit gsplat Gaussians,
and reprojected road RGB and a directional sky panorama. The videos show virtual camera viewpoints and selected original RGB frames.
Browser RGB frames are anonymized source images resized to 768px wide.
The browser uses simple models for traffic and pedestrians, placed from
Mask2Former labels and estimates from RGB camera poses and a ground plane.
The 3D data uses camera RGB and released intrinsics as inputs.
