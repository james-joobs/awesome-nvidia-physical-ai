This is a known-geometry synthetic control, not a learned monocular reconstruction.
The control uses a neutral native SOMA identity and the official example animation.
Horizontal root translation is removed for an in-place display; vertical motion
and official local rotations are retained. Colors are procedural matte values.
The reference RGB is a triangle-mesh render. The interactive model is a
Gaussian approximation driven by the same known skinning matrices.

No frames, audio, inferred motion, geometry, or appearance from the referenced
YouTube clip or any private performer were used in this public control sample.
