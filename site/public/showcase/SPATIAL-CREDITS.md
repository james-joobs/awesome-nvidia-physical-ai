Recorded or rendered on GPU workstation, 2026-11-20. These are our own executions, not downloaded promotional videos. Original takes, full models and intermediate files remain in `${RUN_DIR}` on that server.

**Browser code:** [Spark 2.4.0 by World Labs (MIT)](https://github.com/sparkjsdev/spark) with [Three.js (MIT)](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js). Spark is a renderer, not a redistribution of the Marble model. The browser SPZ is encoded locally with [PlayCanvas splat-transform 3.12.0 (MIT)](https://github.com/playcanvas/splat-transform), retaining all Gaussians and degree-1 color.

**Not executed:** WorldGen's FLUX.1-dev and FLUX.1-Fill-dev weights returned 403 for the server account. NVIDIA Lyra and World Labs Marble are documented reference alternatives; none of the files above is claimed to be a Lyra or Marble generation.
